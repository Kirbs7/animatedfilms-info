What the app does

AnimatedFilms is an owner-operated desktop workflow for researching, producing and preparing educational animated videos. It assembles artwork, narration and captions, checks completed videos, and prepares episode-specific thumbnails and metadata.

With the channel owner's authorization, it connects to YouTube to identify the correct channel, upload videos privately, manage video details and captions, and schedule approved videos for publication. Publishing follows the owner's configured review and scheduling controls.

YouTube account access

The app is intended for the studio's authorized operator, rather than public account registration. Google authorization is required before it can access the connected channel. This informational website does not request Google account access or collect account credentials.

Read our privacy policy for the information the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and how to revoke access.
